Features

Transparent export

ProRes 4444 .mov, WebM VP9 or a PNG sequence with alpha, at your video's exact size and frame rate. Or an .mp4 with the captions burned in.

Works in any editor

EffectCraft, After Effects 2022, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, Final Cut, CapCut.

Offline transcription

Whisper runs on your own computer. Your footage never leaves it.

Timeline

Drag captions to move them, drag their edges to change the duration, cut and join. Double-click to edit. Mark In and Out to export just one part.

Same styles as the plugin

Classic, Center highlight and Karaoke, 7 entrance animations, glow, shadow, stroke, background and the TikTok/Reels safe zone.

Tabs that remember

Work on several videos at once. Close the app and tomorrow everything is right where you left it.

Brands

Switch brands in one click, pin your fonts, paste #HEX/RGB/CMYK codes. Imports the plugin's .sbmarca files.

Install

  1. Download the installer

    A single .exe. No admin rights needed.

  2. Run it

    If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": More info → Run anyway. The app opens by itself when it's done.

  3. Open a video and transcribe

    Drag a video in, hit "Transcribe", fix the text and pick a style.

  4. Export

    Choose ProRes 4444 and put the .mov on top of your video in your editor.

FAQ

How is it different from Captionation?

Captionation is a panel inside Premiere Pro and After Effects 2025/2026. Studio is a standalone app: it doesn't need Adobe and exports the captions as a transparent video you can use in any editor.

Will it run on my older computer?

Yes. It runs on processors without AVX2 (for example Intel Xeon E5 v2 / Ivy Bridge), where recent Adobe apps can't be installed.

Do I need internet?

Only the first time you transcribe: it downloads the transcription model (about 820 MB with the recommended one). If you already use the Captionation panel, it reuses the same model.

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, the app shows "Update now". One click and it installs itself and reopens. Your captions and brands are kept.

Is it really free?

Yes, forever. No subscription, no watermark, no account. Open source under the MIT license — see the code on GitHub.

Windows or my browser warns about the download. Is it safe?

Yes. New free installers without a paid code-signing certificate get flagged until enough people download them. Keep the file, then click More info → Run anyway.
